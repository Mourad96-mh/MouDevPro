## Diagnosis

### The #1 ranking that earns nothing

Combining both spellings, the homepage holds **307 impressions at roughly position 1.2 and
converted 2 clicks — 0.65% CTR.** The unaccented variant sits at position **1.01** — literally the
first organic result — across 111 impressions with **zero** clicks.

At position 1–2, expected CTR is 25–35%. This is a 40× gap and the biggest lost opportunity on the
site. A page does not rank #1 and earn 0.65% unless something is sitting between the ranking and
the user. Three candidates, in order of likelihood:

1. **SERP crowding.** A commercial local query in Morocco returns paid ads plus a local pack above
   organic #1. The organic result may be physically below the fold despite "position 1".
2. **Your own Google Ads listing is taking the click.** `"création site web casablanca"` is an
   active Ads keyword (root `CLAUDE.md` §11). If your ad sits above your own #1 organic result,
   you are paying for clicks the organic listing would earn free. **Check this first** — it is the
   only explanation that costs money while it persists.
3. **Title/meta.** Least likely, since the same title performs adequately on other queries, but
   worth a look once 1 and 2 are ruled out.

Position 1.01 with zero clicks over 111 impressions is not a copy problem. Copy affects CTR by
factors of two, not forty.

### 214 impressions of junk

`internet` (148) and `web hosting` (66) are 17% of query-level impressions and have no commercial
relevance whatsoever. They will never convert, and they depress both CTR and average position.
Nothing to fix on-page — but exclude them mentally when reading the homepage's averages. The
homepage's *commercial* CTR is better than 0.58% suggests, and its *commercial* position is better
than 14.20.

### Cannibalisation, both directions

The homepage **wins** `création site web casablanca` from `/casablanca` (which does not rank for it
at all) and **loses** `développeur web freelance maroc` at 44.47 to four other pages. See
[`_cannibalisation.md`](_cannibalisation.md).

## Actions

1. **Check the Ads/organic overlap on `création site web casablanca`** before changing anything
   on-page. If your ad is cannibalising your own #1 organic result, pausing or lowering that
   keyword may recover clicks at zero cost. *(Do not change the title without re-checking Ads QS
   alignment — root `CLAUDE.md` §5.4 and §10.)*
2. **Then inspect the live SERP** for both spellings from a Casablanca IP. Count what sits above
   the organic #1. If it is 4 ads + a local pack, the fix is Google Business Profile presence, not
   on-page SEO.
3. **Investigate the click decline.** Impressions were flat while clicks halved — that is a CTR or
   SERP-layout change, not a ranking loss. Compare the SERP now against what it looked like in
   June if you have screenshots.
4. **Do not** chase `internet` or `web hosting`.
5. Resolve `développeur web freelance maroc` at the cluster level, not here.
