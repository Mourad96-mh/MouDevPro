## Diagnosis

**The About page outranks both money pages on a commercial keyword.** On `développeur freelance
casablanca` it sits at **13.83**, ahead of `/casablanca` (24.40) and
`/developpeur-web-freelance-maroc` (30.95). That is the wrong page winning — an About page cannot
convert a commercial searcher the way a city or service page can.

This is a symptom of cannibalisation cluster 1, not a cause. See
[`_cannibalisation.md`](_cannibalisation.md). It does tell you something useful though: **the About
page has real topical authority for the freelance-developer entity.** Google associates it strongly
with "développeur freelance". That association is an asset if it is pointed at the right pages, and
a leak if it is not.

The page also surfaces for `développeur web freelance maroc` at 39.14 — again competing with the
page named after that keyword.

Doubling impressions while losing position is the same broad-matching pattern seen on
`/developpeur-web-freelance-maroc` and pre-rewrite `/france`: the page is being shown for more
queries it is a weaker match for.

## Actions

1. **Do not optimise this page for commercial keywords.** It should not be competing for
   `développeur freelance casablanca` at all.
2. **Add prominent keyword-rich internal links** from here to `/casablanca` and
   `/developpeur-web-freelance-maroc`. The page has authority for the entity — route it to the
   pages that convert. This is the cheapest action in cluster 1.
3. **Check the About copy for over-optimised commercial phrasing.** If it reads like a service page,
   soften it toward biography and credibility (experience, process, who you work with) and let the
   money pages carry the keywords.
4. **Keep it indexed.** An About page with genuine E-E-A-T signals supports the whole domain,
   especially for a solo freelancer where the person *is* the brand. Never `noindex` (§10).
5. No re-measurement needed on its own — it will move when cluster 1 is consolidated.
