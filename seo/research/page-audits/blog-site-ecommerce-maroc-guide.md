## Diagnosis

**This post outranks the `/ecommerce` money page on the shared primary keyword.** `site ecommerce
maroc` at **8.00** here versus **28.75** on `/ecommerce` — a 20-place gap in the blog post's favour.

That is a fourth cannibalisation instance, though a milder one than cluster 1: the two pages are not
splitting a large keyword evenly, the blog post is simply winning. And at position 8 it is the
better-placed page for that intent right now.

**Its 2.08% CTR is the second-best on the site** (after `/ar/rabat`'s previous-window 14.29% on 14
impressions). At position 10.74 that is roughly what you would expect — this page is behaving
*normally*, which makes it a useful control: it shows the site's snippets can convert when position
and intent align, strengthening the case that the zero-click pages have specific, fixable problems
rather than a site-wide snippet issue.

**`maroc telecommerce` at 20.67** is a navigational query for the Maroc Telecommerce payment
gateway — a searcher looking for the CMI/payment provider, not for a developer. Low commercial
value directly, but it confirms payment integration is a live concern in this market, which
[`ecommerce.md`](ecommerce.md) also flagged.

**`200 dhs` at position 8** is noise.

## Actions

1. **Add a keyword-rich internal link to `/ecommerce`.** The blog post ranks 8.00 where the money
   page ranks 28.75 — route that traffic. `style-guide.md` §6 requires money pages to receive links
   from page-1 blog posts, and this genuinely is one.
2. **Do not consolidate or redirect it.** It is winning at position 8; leave it.
3. **Build out payment-gateway content** — CMI and Maroc Telecommerce integration. Two separate
   pages in this audit surface payment-related queries, and no page targets it deliberately. Low
   competition, high qualification for Moroccan e-commerce buyers.
4. **Use this page as the CTR benchmark.** 2.08% at position 10.74 is the site's normal. Compare
   the zero-click pages against it rather than against theoretical curves.
5. Re-measure in 4 weeks. Baseline: 96 impressions, 2 clicks, position 10.74.
