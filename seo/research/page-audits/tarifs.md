## Diagnosis

**This is the most commercially damaging trend in the audit.** Price-intent searchers are the
closest to converting of any organic traffic, and this page lost two-thirds of its visibility in
28 days.

Its primary keyword `prix site web maroc` still ranks 8.67 — page 1 — but on only 3 recorded
impressions, down from a page averaging 8.61 across 74. The impressions did not move to another
page: `/blog/prix-site-web-maroc-2026` fell in the same window (69 → 9 impressions, position 8.12 →
13.78). **Both price pages declined together**, which points at something affecting the price
cluster as a whole rather than a page-specific problem.

Plausible causes, none confirmable from GSC alone:

- A competitor gained ground on the price cluster.
- Google changed what it surfaces for Moroccan price queries (AI Overviews increasingly absorb
  "prix" and "combien coûte" queries and suppress clicks to the pages they cite).
- Seasonal demand drop across the price cluster.

**Nothing in this audit's data distinguishes between these.** That is worth stating plainly rather
than guessing — the fix differs completely depending on which it is.

## Actions

1. **Confirm the trend over a longer window** before acting. Pull 3–6 months for `/tarifs` and
   `/blog/prix-site-web-maroc-2026` together. A 28-day comparison can be noise; two correlated
   declines over 90 days would not be.
2. **Check the live SERP for `prix site web maroc`** — specifically whether an AI Overview now
   occupies the top of the page. If so, this is an industry-wide shift and the response is
   structured data and citable content, not conventional on-page work.
3. **Verify Ads landing-page health.** Four active Ads keywords point here or share it with
   `/casablanca`. Organic decline does not affect Quality Score, but confirm the page still meets
   §5.4 (keyword in title, H1, first 100 words).
4. **Do not rewrite the page yet.** With 9 query-level impressions there is not enough signal to
   target anything new. Diagnose first.
5. Priority: **high urgency to investigate, low urgency to change.**
