## Reading

- **Every keyword checked has paid competitors**: commercial intent is confirmed for all 12.
- **Same core rivals everywhere:** creaweb.ma (in all 12), dotma.ma, mediazain.com, fes-marketing.net, technopek.com.
  Price keywords also attract nerolinkmedia.com, majormedia.marketing, rhillane.com, agenceflash.com.
- **Price keywords are where we're weakest:** "prix site web casablanca" IS 31%, [prix site web maroc] 47%.
  These are the most purchase-ready searches (persona 4) and the most contested.
- **"développeur web" / "web developer casablanca":** a job board (marocemploiit.com) bids too, so some
  searchers are job-seekers, not clients. Watch the search terms; the job negatives list already exists.
- Platforms (shopify, strikingly, hostinger, godaddy, rankwebsitebuilder) show up on generic terms: those
  searchers may want DIY builders, not a developer.
