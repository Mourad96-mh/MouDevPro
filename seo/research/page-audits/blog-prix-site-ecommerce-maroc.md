## Diagnosis

**The one price page that is working.** While `/tarifs` (8.61 → 19.85) and
`/blog/prix-site-web-maroc-2026` (8.12 → 13.78) both collapsed this window, this post entered at
position 10.20 and converted.

That is genuinely useful evidence for the price-cluster question raised in
[`tarifs.md`](tarifs.md) and [`blog-prix-site-web-maroc-2026.md`](blog-prix-site-web-maroc-2026.md).
**If a broad force were suppressing Moroccan price queries — an AI Overview rollout, say — a brand
new price page would not enter at position 10 and earn a click.** It weakens the AI Overview
hypothesis and shifts weight toward competitor movement or ordinary volatility on the two specific
pages that fell.

It is not conclusive: this post targets *e-commerce* pricing while the two that fell target general
website pricing. Different query sets could behave differently. But it is the strongest
counter-evidence available and should be weighed before concluding the price cluster is structurally
damaged.

**`prix développement application web maroc` at 40.00** is the same query `/tarifs` surfaces for at
77.00 — both weak. Application/custom-development pricing is unclaimed by any page on the site;
root `CLAUDE.md` §6 lists "application web sur mesure" as one of the three `/tarifs` tiers, but
nothing ranks for it.

## Actions

1. **Use this page as evidence in the price-cluster diagnosis.** Before assuming a structural cause
   for the `/tarifs` decline, account for the fact that a new price page entered at position 10 in
   the same window.
2. **Add a keyword-rich internal link to `/tarifs`** — it ranks 10.20 while `/tarifs` sits at 19.85,
   so it is currently the better-placed page for e-commerce price intent and should feed the money
   page.

   `prix développement application web maroc` at 40 and 77; nothing owns it. `/tarifs` already has
   an "application web sur mesure" tier per §6.
4. **Watch for one more window before drawing conclusions** — 56 impressions and 1 click is a small
   base, and 4% query coverage means the composition is unknown.
5. Baseline: 56 impressions, 1 click, position 10.20.
