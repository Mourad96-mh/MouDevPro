## Diagnosis

**Healthy growth, still stuck on page 3.** The primary keyword `site e-commerce maroc` appears in
three spellings (`site e commerce maroc`, `site ecommerce maroc`, plus the hyphenated form in
withheld data) at positions 28–31. Nothing on page 1.

**The low query coverage is itself informative.** 33% coverage means this page attracts a wide,
thin long tail rather than a few concentrated head terms — typical for e-commerce service queries,
where searchers describe their specific situation. That is a content-depth opportunity: broad
topical coverage tends to reward pages that answer many adjacent questions.

**Three specific signals worth acting on:**

- **`vendre en ligne maroc` at 23.20** is the page's best position and is *not* a "site e-commerce"
  phrasing. It is outcome-framed — the searcher wants to sell online, not to buy a website. Root
  `CLAUDE.md` §8 explicitly prescribes leading with the outcome; this query confirms the audience
  thinks that way.
- **`woocommerce maroc` at 52.00** — the platform is named in the §8 differentiator phrases
  ("React, WordPress, WooCommerce — selon vos besoins") but the page is barely competitive for it.
  Platform-specific queries are lower-volume and much lower-competition.
- **`متجر الكتروني المغرب` at 53.25** — Arabic e-commerce demand hitting the **French** page. The
  Arabic pages (`/ar/`, `/ar/rabat`, `/ar/marrakech`) do not cover e-commerce at all, and §5.2
  lists `متجر إلكتروني المغرب` as a target. There is no Arabic e-commerce page to send this to.

## Actions

1. **Add outcome-framed copy around `vendre en ligne`** — its best-ranking term and its most
   natural framing for the audience.
2. **Build out platform coverage** — WooCommerce, and CMI payment integration which is the standard
   Moroccan concern. Low competition, high qualification.
3. **Consider Arabic e-commerce coverage.** §5.2 targets `متجر إلكتروني المغرب`; the query is
   landing on the French page at position 53. Per `style-guide.md`, you are the only structured
   Arabic competitor in this market — that advantage is unused here.
4. **Do not change the slug or the `#ecommerce` anchor** — four active Ads keywords and §10
   protects the anchor.
5. Re-measure in 4 weeks. Baseline: 218 impressions, 3 clicks, position 25.45.
