## Diagnosis

**This post competes with the homepage on the site's most valuable keyword and loses by 16
positions** — 17.45 versus 1.42. See [`_cannibalisation.md`](_cannibalisation.md) cluster 2.

That is not necessarily bad. The homepage holds #1; this post adds 137 impressions of secondary
coverage on the same term. It is not displacing the homepage, and there is no evidence it is
holding the homepage back.

**Its genuinely useful rankings are the long-tail variants the homepage does not hold:**

- `développement web sur mesure à casablanca` at **9.67** — page 1, and notably this is the *only*
  page on the site that ranks respectably for any `sur mesure` phrasing. `/france` (97) and
  `/services` (82) both fail on the un-geo'd version; adding "à casablanca" makes it winnable.
- `création site web casablanca prix` at **13.67** — price intent, and it belongs to `/tarifs` by
  §5.1. With `/tarifs` having fallen to position 19.85, this post is currently the better-placed
  page for Casablanca price intent.
- `developpement site web casablanca` at **12.78** — unaccented, no-hyphen variant.

**Zero clicks on 403 impressions across two windows** is a real problem independent of ranking.
Positions 9.67–20 should produce some clicks. Nothing at all suggests the snippet is not competing
— the same conclusion reached on `/blog/site-web-hotel-riad-marrakech`, though here the positions
are weaker so the expected click count is lower.

## Actions

1. **Keep it. Do not consolidate it into `/casablanca`.** It holds three page-1-adjacent long-tail
   positions the money pages do not.
2. **Add a keyword-rich internal link to `/casablanca`.** `style-guide.md` §6 requires money pages
   to receive links from page-1 blog posts. This post is the closest thing to one for the Casablanca
   cluster, and `/casablanca` needs the help — it does not rank for its own primary keyword.
3. **Link to `/tarifs` on the price query.** This post ranks 13.67 for `création site web casablanca
   prix` while `/tarifs` has fallen to 19.85 — route that intent to the pricing page.
4. **Review the title and meta description.** Zero clicks on 403 impressions warrants it, though
   with lower expected CTR than the riad post this is second priority.
5. **Note the `sur mesure` lesson.** Geo-qualifying the term makes it winnable (9.67 here vs 82–97
   elsewhere). Relevant to `/services`, which is currently losing on the un-geo'd version.
