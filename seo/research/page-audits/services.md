## Diagnosis

**Neither §5.1 target keyword appears in the data.** `site vitrine maroc` and `refonte site web
maroc` — the two terms this page exists to own — generate no recorded impressions at all. The page
is not competing for its assigned keywords in any measurable way.

**What it does surface for is unwinnable.** `développement web sur mesure` at position 82 is the
same generic head term that was poisoning `/france` before its rewrite (89 impressions at 97 there).
Two pages on this site are being matched to that term and both rank in the 80–100 range. It is the
most contested phrase in French web development and neither page has any prospect on it.

`développeur web freelance maroc` at **position 134** is the seventh page in cannibalisation
cluster 1 and by far the weakest — see [`_cannibalisation.md`](_cannibalisation.md).

The second query is a long concatenated string of service names (`développement web · hébergement ·
tunnel de vente · design graphique · SEO · SEA · IA · maintenance · freelance · optimisation`).
That is someone's copy-pasted service list, not a real search. Its presence is a mild signal that
this page reads as an undifferentiated list of services — matching a query that is itself an
undifferentiated list.

**With 19 impressions this page is effectively invisible.** It is the weakest money page on the
site by a wide margin.

## Actions

1. **Decide whether this page should exist as a ranking target at all.** It has assigned keywords it
   does not compete for and 19 impressions across 28 days. Options: invest properly in it, or accept
   it as a navigational hub and move `site vitrine maroc` / `refonte site web maroc` onto pages
   with existing authority.
2. **If keeping it: target `refonte site web maroc` specifically.** Redesign/refonte intent is
   distinct, commercially strong, and not covered by any other page on the site — no other page in
   this audit surfaces for it. That is unclaimed ground rather than a fight.
3. **Remove `développeur web freelance maroc` from its targeting** (cluster 1) and stop competing
   for `développement web sur mesure` — both are lost causes here.
4. **Investigate the 43-place drop** alongside `/contact` and `/tarifs`. Three pages fell sharply in
   the same window; that pattern deserves one investigation, not three.
5. **Do not remove the `#tarifs` / `#ecommerce` / `#freelance` anchors** if present — §10 protects
   them for Ads.
