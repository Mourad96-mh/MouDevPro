## Diagnosis

**This page ranks for Morocco-level English terms, not Rabat-level ones.** Not a single query in
its visible data mentions Rabat. It surfaces for `web developer morocco`, `web development company
morocco`, `web development agency morocco` — national English queries that `/en/` is also competing
for, and ranking better on (`/en/` holds `web designer morocco` at 20.03).

So the English city pages are duplicating each other's targeting rather than splitting it by city.
`/en/` and `/en/rabat/` compete on the same national English cluster, and `/en/` wins.

**`web developer morocco` at 43.45 is a §5.2 target** and its best non-brand position — but it is a
national term that logically belongs to `/en/`, not to a Rabat page.

**Three of six queries are agency-framed** (`web development company morocco`, `web development
company`, `web dev agency`, `web development agency morocco` — 23 impressions at positions 62–80).
Same pattern as `/en/`: the English pages attract agency searches at unwinnable positions. Root
`CLAUDE.md` §2 and §8 say this site is explicitly not an agency; the English copy may be
contradicting that.

**`moudev` at 5.33** is a brand search — someone looking for you specifically landing on the English
Rabat page rather than the homepage. Minor, but it suggests brand queries are being distributed
oddly.

## Actions

1. **Fix the trailing-slash duplication** — this page's data is split across two URLs.
2. **Give it actual Rabat targeting or reconsider it.** An English Rabat page that never surfaces
   for a Rabat query is not doing the job its URL implies. Either add genuine Rabat-specific English
   content (institutional, NGO, startup framing per §6) or accept it as a thin variant.
3. **Stop competing with `/en/` on national English terms.** `/en/` ranks better on the same
   cluster; this page should not duplicate it.
4. **Audit the English copy for "agency" / "company" phrasing** — same recommendation as `/en/`,
   and the query data on both pages points the same way.
5. Low priority relative to `/en/`, which has 6× the impressions on the same keyword family.
