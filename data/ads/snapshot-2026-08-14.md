## Reading the change history

Two patterns matter, and both need the `client_type` column to see:

**1. `GOOGLE_ADS_MOBILE_APP` — 55 events, manual.** A twice-daily campaign pause/resume cycle at
roughly 00:30 and 08:40, every day, on both live campaigns. 53 of the 150 events are campaign
status changes. All campaigns run `MAXIMIZE_CONVERSIONS`, so the bid model is being restarted
daily.

**2. `GOOGLE_ADS_RECOMMENDATIONS` — Google changing the account by itself.** One row:

```
2026-08-06 21:37:25  Creation-site-web  CAMPAIGN  UPDATE
    GOOGLE_ADS_RECOMMENDATIONS   maximizeConversions.targetCpaMicros
```

**Auto-apply recommendations is switched on**, and Google set a target CPA on the worst-performing
campaign during the week its CPC tripled. Separately, 86 of the 150 events are `ASSET / CREATE`
from `INTERNAL_TOOL` — automatically created ad assets, also generated by Google rather than by
you.

Neither is inherently wrong, but both mean the account changes without anyone deciding to change
it, which makes any before/after measurement unreliable. Check
**Recommendations → auto-apply settings** before running any test.

## Known gap

**Auction Insights is not exposed by the Google Ads API** at any version — verified by probe, not
assumed:

```
queryError: UNRECOGNIZED_FIELD
"Unrecognized field in the query: 'auction_insight_domain.domain'."
```

Competitor bidding data must be exported by hand from Campaigns → Insights & reports → Auction
insights, and dropped into this folder as `auction-insights-<date>.csv`.
