## Diagnosis

**The page does not rank for its own primary keyword.** `création site web casablanca` — the term
in its title, H1 and Ads targeting — does not appear in its query list at all. The homepage owns
that term at position 1.42; see [`_cannibalisation.md`](_cannibalisation.md) cluster 2.

What the page *does* rank for is the developer-oriented cluster: `développeur freelance casablanca`
at 24.40 (its strongest term by far, 53 impressions) and `développeur web casablanca` at 36.43.
Both are legitimate §5.1 targets for this page — it is simply winning the second half of its
keyword set and losing the first half to its own homepage.

**Two of its terms are outranked by pages that should not be competing:** `/about` beats it on
`développeur freelance casablanca` (13.83 vs 24.40), and `/developpeur-web-freelance-maroc` sits
between them at 30.95.

**Position 45–74 on `développeur web à casablanca` and `développement web casablanca`** — near-
identical phrasings of terms it ranks 24–36 for — suggests the page's on-page targeting is narrow.
It matches one phrasing well and its close variants poorly.

## Actions

1. **Accept the split with the homepage** (Option A in `_cannibalisation.md`). Do not try to take
   `création site web casablanca` back — it would mean displacing your own #1.
2. **Retarget this page on the developer cluster it already wins.** `développeur freelance
   casablanca` at 24.40 with 53 impressions is a page-3 ranking on a commercially strong term; it
   is the most realistic page-1 candidate on the whole site outside the blog.
3. **Broaden phrasing coverage** — work `développement web casablanca` and `développeur web à
   casablanca` naturally into body copy. These are the same intent at 45–74 while their sibling
   phrasing ranks 24.
4. **Resolve `/about` outranking it** — see cluster 1.
5. **Ads note:** this page continues to serve six active Ads keywords. Its organic weakness on
   `création site web casablanca` does **not** affect Quality Score, which is judged on page
   content, not ranking. The §5.4 requirement (keyword in title, H1, first 100 words) is
   independent of this finding — do not remove the keyword from the page.
