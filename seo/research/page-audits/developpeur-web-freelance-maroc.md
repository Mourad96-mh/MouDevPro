## Diagnosis

**This page ranks third for its own name.** On `développeur web freelance maroc` it sits at 36.78,
behind `/blog/freelance-creation-site-web-maroc` (26.95) and
`/blog/choisir-developpeur-web-freelance-maroc` (31.96). Two blog posts outrank the dedicated money
page for the exact term it was built to own.

This is the clearest instance of cannibalisation cluster 1 — see
[`_cannibalisation.md`](_cannibalisation.md). Seven pages compete for this keyword family and none
reaches page 1. The money page should be consolidating those signals and is instead splitting them.

**Its best position is on the unaccented spelling** — `developpeur web freelance` at 24.24, twelve
places better than the accented form at 35.58. The URL slug is unaccented, which likely explains
it. Worth noting that Moroccan users search both ways heavily.

**The impression explosion is not good news.** Growing from 32 to 335 impressions while dropping to
position 46 means the page is being shown for a wider set of queries it ranks poorly for —
`développement web maroc` at 63.81, `développeur web maroc` at 56.25. Same pattern as `/france`
before its rewrite: broad matching to generic national terms that inflate impressions and depress
averages without ever converting.

## Actions

1. **Make this the consolidation target for cluster 1.** It has the right name, the right slug, and
   the right intent. Everything in the cluster should point here.
2. **Add internal links from both blog posts** with keyword-rich anchors — `style-guide.md` §6
   requires money pages to receive links from page-1 blog posts, and both currently outrank this
   page instead of feeding it.
3. **Differentiate the blog posts' titles and H1s** so they stop competing on the exact term. Their
   angles ("comment choisir", "freelance vs agence") are distinct; their titles are not distinct
   enough.
4. **Remove this keyword from `/about` and `/services` targeting** — both surface for it (39.14 and
   134) and neither should.
5. **Cover both spellings** — the unaccented form ranks 12 places better and is not weaker demand.
6. Re-measure after the cluster is consolidated. Baseline: 335 impressions, 1 click, position 46.39,
   best cluster position 26.95.
